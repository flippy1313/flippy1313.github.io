About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Duke University, advised by Dr. Lisa Wu Wills in the APEX Lab.

My research focuses on efficient LLM systems and the hardware and software components that support them. My primary work characterizes end-to-end retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and develops low-bit KV-cache compression and storage techniques for long-context LLM serving. I use workload characterization, hardware–software co-design, and specialized arithmetic to improve the efficiency and scalability of LLM systems.

Previously, I earned master’s degrees in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Data Science, and a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics with a double major in Data Science, at the University of Michigan. During my master’s studies, I worked with Dr. Reetuparna Das.
